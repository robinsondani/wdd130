Exercises

Overview: This page lists common therapeutic exercises grouped by body region (e.g., shoulder, knee, lower back) and includes clear instructions, recommended repetitions, and safety modifications. Each exercise entry will explain the goal, the step-by-step movement, and common form cues.

Example Exercise Copy: Glute Bridge — Purpose: activate the gluteal muscles and improve pelvic stability. Steps: lie on your back with knees bent, press through heels, lift hips to form a straight line from shoulders to knees, hold 2–3 seconds, lower slowly. Reps: 2–3 sets of 10–15. Modifications: place hands on hips for feedback; progress by adding a heel raise or single-leg bridge.
