Exercises Included:

    Purpose: Strengthens the quadriceps without bending the knee.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps per leg, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Lie on your back with one knee bent and the other leg straight.
    2. Tighten the thigh muscle of the straight leg.
    3. Lift the straight leg to the height of the bent knee.
    4. Lower slowly with control.

    Purpose: Strengthens the glute medius and hip abductors.

    Recommended Reps: 12–15 reps per side, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Lie on your side with knees bent and feet together.
    2. Keep your hips stacked and core engaged.
    3. Lift your top knee while keeping your feet touching.
    4. Lower slowly without rolling your hips backward.

    Purpose: Strengthens glutes and improves pelvic stability.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat.
    2. Squeeze your glutes and lift your hips upward.
    3. Hold for 2–3 seconds at the top.
    4. Lower slowly back to the floor.

    Purpose: Improves knee mobility and gently activates hamstrings.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Lie on your back with legs extended.
    2. Slide one heel toward your glutes as far as comfortable.
    3. Pause briefly, then slide the heel back out.
    4. Repeat with control.

    Purpose: Strengthens quads and glutes with low joint stress.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Stand with feet hip-width apart.
    2. Bend your knees slightly into a quarter squat.
    3. Keep your chest lifted and weight in your heels.
    4. Return to standing with control.
