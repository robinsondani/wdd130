Exercises Included:

  • Purpose: Strengthens the quadriceps without bending the knee.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps per leg, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Lie on your back with one knee bent and the other leg straight.
    2. Tighten the thigh muscle of the straight leg.
    3. Lift the straight leg to the height of the bent knee.
    4. Lower slowly with control.

  • Purpose: Strengthens the glute medius and hip abductors.

    Recommended Reps: 12–15 reps per side, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Lie on your side with knees bent and feet together.
    2. Keep your hips stacked and core engaged.
    3. Lift your top knee while keeping your feet touching.
    4. Lower slowly without rolling your hips backward.

  • Purpose: Strengthens glutes and improves pelvic stability.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat.
    2. Squeeze your glutes and lift your hips upward.
    3. Hold for 2–3 seconds at the top.
    4. Lower slowly back to the floor.

  • Purpose: Improves knee mobility and gently activates hamstrings.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Lie on your back with legs extended.
    2. Slide one heel toward your glutes as far as comfortable.
    3. Pause briefly, then slide the heel back out.
    4. Repeat with control.

  • Purpose: Strengthens quads and glutes with low joint stress.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Stand with feet hip-width apart.
    2. Bend your knees slightly into a quarter squat.
    3. Keep your chest lifted and weight in your heels.
    4. Return to standing with control.

Exercises Included:

  • Purpose: Improves ankle mobility and control.

    Recommended Reps: 1–2 rounds of the alphabet

    Steps:

    1. Sit comfortably with your leg extended.
    2. Lift your foot slightly off the ground.
    3. Use your toes to “draw” the alphabet in the air.

  • Purpose: Strengthens calves and improves ankle stability.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Stand tall with feet hip-width apart.
    2. Lift your heels off the ground.
    3. Pause briefly at the top.
    4. Lower slowly with control.

  • Purpose: Strengthens the small muscles of the foot.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 scrunches, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Place a towel on the floor in front of you.
    2. Use your toes to pull the towel toward you.
    3. Reset and repeat.

Exercises Included:

  • Purpose: Improves balance and ankle stability.

    Recommended Reps: Hold 20–30 seconds per leg, 2–3 rounds

    Steps:

    1. Stand tall and lift one foot off the ground.
    2. Keep hips level and core engaged.
    3. Hold steady without wobbling.

  • Purpose: Improves balance and gait control.

    Recommended Reps: 10–20 steps, 2–3 rounds

    Steps:

    1. Walk forward placing one foot directly in front of the other.
    2. Keep your steps slow and controlled.
    3. Use a wall for support if needed.

Exercises Included:

  • Purpose: Activates deep core muscles and improves lumbar mobility.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Lie on your back with knees bent.
    2. Flatten your lower back into the floor by tilting your pelvis.
    3. Hold briefly, then release.

  • Purpose: Strengthens core and back stabilizers.

    Recommended Reps: 10 reps per side, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Start on hands and knees.
    2. Extend opposite arm and leg.
    3. Keep hips level and core tight.
    4. Return and switch sides.

  • Purpose: Builds deep core control and coordination.

    Recommended Reps: 10–12 reps per side, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Lie on your back with arms up and knees at 90°.
    2. Lower opposite arm and leg toward the floor.
    3. Return to the starting position.
    4. Switch sides.

Exercises Included:

  • Purpose: Improves posture and shoulder mobility.

    Recommended Reps: 10–12 reps, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Stand with your back against a wall.
    2. Place arms in a “W” shape.
    3. Slide arms upward into a “Y” shape.
    4. Return to the starting position.

  • Purpose: Strengthens upper back and shoulder stabilizers.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Sit or stand tall.
    2. Gently squeeze your shoulder blades together.
    3. Hold briefly, then release.

  • Purpose: Strengthens the rotator cuff.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps per arm, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Hold a resistance band with elbow bent at 90°.
    2. Rotate your forearm outward away from your body.
    3. Return slowly to the starting position.

  • Purpose: Provides gentle shoulder mobility.

    Recommended Reps: 20–30 seconds each direction

    Steps:

    1. Lean forward and let your arm hang.
    2. Gently swing your arm in circles.
    3. Switch directions.

Exercises Included:

  • Purpose: Improves hamstring flexibility.

    Recommended Reps: Hold 20–30 seconds, 2–3 rounds

    Steps:

    1. Lie on your back and lift one leg.
    2. Hold behind your thigh.
    3. Gently straighten your knee until you feel a stretch.

  • Purpose: Improves spinal mobility.

    Recommended Reps: 10–15 reps, 2–3 sets

    Steps:

    1. Start on hands and knees.
    2. Arch your back upward (Cat).
    3. Drop your belly and lift your chest (Cow).

  • Purpose: Reduces neck tension and improves flexibility.

    Recommended Reps: Hold 20–30 seconds per side, 2–3 rounds

    Steps:

    1. Sit or stand tall.
    2. Gently tilt your head to one side.
    3. Feel the stretch along the opposite side of your neck.
