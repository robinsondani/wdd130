Recovery

Overview: The Recovery page offers information about pacing, pain vs. harm, sleep and nutrition tips for healing, and suggestions for when to contact a healthcare provider. It will also include links to printable progress logs and reputable external resources.

Sample Recovery Tip: Sleep and consistency are powerful recovery tools. Aim for regular sleep windows, hydrate well, and include gentle mobility in your daily routine. If pain increases sharply or you experience new neurological symptoms, stop the activity and contact your provider.
