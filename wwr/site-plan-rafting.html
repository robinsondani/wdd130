Overview

Purpose

Audience Need: Dry Oar Boating is a recreational website meant to showcase white‑water rafting vacations, help visitors explore river options, and ultimately book an adventure. It provides vivid images, trip summaries, logistical details, and easy contact/booking information so users can plan their next rafting getaway.

What Visitors Want: Visitors will be looking for thrilling but safe river choices, quick comparisons between trips, inspiring photos of rapids and campsites, and a straightforward way to get in touch or reserve space. They expect responsive design, clean navigation, and persuasive copy that conveys excitement without overwhelming them.

Primary Tasks: Users should be able to learn about available rivers and trips, see images and descriptions, navigate to a contact/booking form, and access the site plan or other informational pages. Returning users may simply want to revisit a favorite river or check updates.

Why This Site: Dry Oar Boating stands apart by combining adventure‑focused storytelling with usability; each river page will highlight unique features, difficulty ratings, and camping options while keeping content scannable. The site is designed to inspire new rafters and provide clear next steps for booking and questions.

Content Structure: The site will have a home page, a rivers listing/detail page, a contact/booking page, and this site plan. Navigation between pages will be consistent and simple. Images will be optimized, and text will be concise with bold calls to action.

Tone & Accessibility: The voice will be upbeat, welcoming, and reassuring. Text will use accessible fonts and contrast, images will include descriptive alt text, and layout will adapt to mobile devices.

Audience

The target audience includes adventure seekers, families, and groups planning a rafting trip. They range from first‑time rafters looking for excitement to experienced paddlers comparing river difficulty and scenery. The site also serves as a referral for guides and outdoor clubs.

Content

Home page

Elevator Pitch: Dry Oar Boating provides thrilling white‑water rafting vacations with expert guides, beautiful campsites, and unforgettable scenery. The home page invites visitors to "Have An Adventure" and points them to rivers, booking, and the site plan for more information.

Welcome Message: Welcome to Dry Oar Boating! Browse our river options, see what makes each trip unique, and contact us to reserve your spot. We make planning easy so you can focus on the fun.

Rivers

Overview: This page lists the rivers we run with a short description, difficulty level, length of trip, and available dates. Visitors can click through for details on each river or scroll to compare choices quickly.

Example Copy: Snake River — Class III–IV rapids winding through a canyon with stunning rock formations. Trips include two nights of camping, guided instruction, and optional inflatable kayaks. Perfect for intermediate paddlers and families with teens.

Contact

Overview: The contact page features a simple booking form, phone number, email, and a map to our headquarters. It also includes a short FAQ section about cancellations, gear, and group discounts.

Sample FAQ Item: What should I pack? Bring quick‑dry clothing, a waterproof bag, sunscreen, and a sense of adventure. We supply helmets, PFDs, and paddles.
